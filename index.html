Why Scout?

Finding internships, scholarships, and programs as a high school student is overwhelming. Opportunities are spread across different websites, deadlines are easy to miss, and many students don't even know what exists.

Scout solves this by curating high-quality opportunities in one place, helping students discover, track, and apply with confidence.

Free forever Works offline No API key Local matching

Built by Reece Scott-Hansen, a high school student interested in business, finance, technology, and helping students access better opportunities.

Founder Story

R
Reece Scott-Hansen
Founder · 11th Grade · NYC

Scout started as a project built by a high school student interested in business, finance, technology, and helping students find better opportunities. While looking for internships, scholarships, programs, and competitions, I noticed that great opportunities were scattered across different websites and easy to miss.

I built Scout to make that process simpler. The goal is to give students one clean place to discover opportunities, save what matters, track deadlines, and share useful programs with friends.
